Easy Reci — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Easy Reci is a Hong Kong recipe app. It collects supermarket discounts each day and suggests recipes built from what is actually on offer. This policy explains what the app stores about you, why, and who else sees it. It is written to describe what the software genuinely does, not to cover every theoretical possibility.

What we collect

Only if you create an account. You can browse daily recipes and supermarket deals without signing in, and if you never create an account we hold nothing about you.

When you do sign up, we store:

  • Your email address, and your name if you provide one
  • A one-way hash of your password — never the password itself. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we store only the identifier that service gives us
  • Your dietary preferences and language choice
  • The recipes you mark as favourites
  • Your subscription status, and a count of how many cloud recipe generations you have used in the current period, so free-tier limits can be applied

What we do not collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, or health data
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA). The app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it never asks to track you
  • Analytics about how you move around the app
  • Payment card details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we only learn whether a subscription is active

Recipe generation and AI

The app can generate recipes two ways, and they are meaningfully different for your privacy.

On your device. On supported iPhones, generation runs through Apple's on-device foundation models. Your request never leaves the phone and we never see it.

In the cloud. If you choose cloud generation, the text of your request — for example "something with chicken wings and potatoes" — is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the recipe, along with the current supermarket items it should use. Do not put personal information into that box; it is sent to Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. They are non-personalised: AdMob is asked for ads based only on the kind of content in the app, such as cooking and groceries, and the app does not request or use your advertising identifier. Google still receives the technical information needed to serve an ad, such as your approximate region and device type. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Supermarket data

Discount information shown in the app is collected from publicly available Hong Kong supermarket websites. It is about products, not about you, and browsing it involves no personal data at all.

Where your information is stored

Account data is held in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted on Microsoft Azure in the Southeast Asia region (Singapore). If you are in Hong Kong, your information is therefore stored outside Hong Kong.

Who else sees it

  • Apple — sign-in, subscriptions and app distribution
  • Google — Sign-In if you use it, AdMob for ads, and Gemini for cloud recipe generation
  • Microsoft Azure — hosting of the service and database

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Keeping it secure

  • All traffic between the app and our service uses HTTPS
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device
  • Email addresses and phone numbers are masked in our server logs

No service can promise perfect security. These are the measures the app actually implements, described plainly so you can judge them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, under Settings. Deletion removes your account record, your favourites and your preferences from our database. Some information may persist briefly in routine backups before being overwritten.

Children

Easy Reci is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who we share it with, the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests to access or delete your data: privacy@easyreci.com
